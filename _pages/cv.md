## Work in Progress

- **Being Tough on Crime: Effects on School Dropout**
  - This paper studies the effects of Honduras' Mano Dura policy on school dropout.
  - Preliminary results suggest that the crackdown reduced overall dropout by around 40% relative to the pre-treatment mean.
